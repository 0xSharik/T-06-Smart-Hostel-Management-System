# Code of Conduct

Contributors, mentors, and reviewers are expected to communicate respectfully and professionally. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, deliberate disruption, plagiarism, fabricated evidence, and disclosure of private student/visitor information are not acceptable.

Give constructive feedback focused on the work. Acknowledge original authors, third-party code, packages, and assets. Raise concerns privately with the project maintainers or faculty mentor. Project maintainers should review reports promptly, protect reporter privacy where possible, and take proportionate steps to keep participation safe and respectful.
